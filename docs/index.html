📌 What is DHS 2026-14439?

📌 什么是DHS 2026-14439？

DHS 2026-14439 is a final rule that eliminates the "Duration of Status" (D/S) framework for F-1 students, J-1 exchange visitors, and I-category representatives. Starting September 15, 2026, these nonimmigrants will be admitted for fixed time periods (maximum 4 years for F/J, 240 days for I) instead of indefinite stays. Students and exchange visitors who need additional time must now apply directly to USCIS for an Extension of Stay (EOS).

DHS 2026-14439是一项最终规则，消除了F-1学生、J-1交流访问者和I类代表的"身份存续期"(D/S)框架。 从2026年9月15日开始，这些非移民将以固定时间期限入境（F/J类最长4年，I类240天），而不是无限期停留。 需要额外时间的学生和交流访问者现在必须直接向USCIS申请停留延期(EOS)。

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

❓ 常见问题

Q: Who does this rule affect?

问：这项规则影响谁？

F-1 students, J-1 exchange visitors, and I-category media representatives. The rule replaces indefinite "Duration of Status" with fixed time periods.

F-1学生、J-1交流访问者和I类媒体代表。该规则将无限期的"身份存续期"替换为固定时间期限。

Q: What if I need more time than the fixed period?

问：如果我需要的时间超过固定期限怎么办？

You must file a formal Extension of Stay (EOS) application with USCIS before your current period expires. Simply continuing to study or work is not sufficient.

你必须在当前期限到期前向USCIS提交正式的停留延期(EOS)申请。仅仅继续学习或工作是不够的。

Q: What happens if I overstay?

问：如果我逾期居留会怎样？

Unlawful presence automatically begins the day after your admission period expires. As little as 180 days of unlawful presence triggers a 3-year bar; over 1 year triggers a 10-year bar, affecting future visa applications.

无证居留在你的入境期限过期后的次日自动开始。仅需180天的无证居留就会触发3年禁入；超过1年会触发10年禁入，影响未来的签证申请。

Q: When does this rule take effect?

问：这项规则何时生效？

September 15, 2026 for new arrivals. Existing students/visitors have a transition period until November 14, 2030 (F-1) or October 15, 2030 (J-1), after which the new rules apply to everyone.

对于新入境者，生效日期为2026年9月15日。现有学生/访问者有过渡期至2030年11月14日（F-1）或2030年10月15日（J-1），之后新规则适用于所有人。
