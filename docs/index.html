What is DHS 2026-14439?

DHS 2026-14439 是什么?

A final rule that eliminates the "Duration of Status" (D/S) framework for F-1 students, J-1 exchange visitors, and I-category media representatives. Starting September 15, 2026, these nonimmigrants are admitted for fixed time periods (max 4 years for F/J, 240 days for I) instead of indefinite stays. Anyone needing more time must file an Extension of Stay (EOS) with USCIS.

该最终规则取消了 F-1 学生、J-1 交流访问学者及 I 类媒体代表适用的"身份存续期"(D/S) 框架。自 2026 年 9 月 15 日起,上述非移民将按固定期限入境(F/J 最长 4 年、I 类 240 天),不再无限期停留。如需更长时间,必须直接向 USCIS 申请停留延期(EOS)。

Frequently asked questions

常见问题

Who does this rule affect? 这项规则影响谁?

F-1 students, J-1 exchange visitors, and I-category media representatives — plus their F-2 / J-2 dependents. The rule replaces indefinite "Duration of Status" with fixed admission periods.

F-1 学生、J-1 交流访问学者及 I 类媒体代表,以及他们的 F-2 / J-2 家属。规则以固定期限入境取代了原本无限期的"身份存续期"。

What if I need more time than my fixed period? 如果需要比固定期限更长的时间怎么办?

File a formal Extension of Stay (EOS) with USCIS before your current period expires. Simply continuing to study or work is not sufficient — timely filing is required.

必须在当前期限到期之前向 USCIS 提交正式的停留延期(EOS)申请。仅仅继续学习或工作并不够 — 必须按时提交申请。

What happens if I overstay? 如果逾期居留会怎样?

Unlawful presence begins the day after your admission period expires. As little as 180 days triggers a 3-year bar; over 1 year triggers a 10-year bar — both affect future visa applications.

非法居留从入境期限到期后次日起算。累计 180 天即触发 3 年禁入,超过 1 年则触发 10 年禁入 — 都将影响未来的签证申请。

Is there a transition period for people already here? 已经在美的人有过渡期吗?

Yes. Existing students and visitors keep status until November 14, 2030 (F-1) or October 15, 2030 (J-1), after which the new rules apply to everyone.

有。现有学生与访问者可维持身份至 2030 年 11 月 14 日(F-1)或 2030 年 10 月 15 日(J-1),之后新规则适用于所有人。
